Rizal Life

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Rizal Life is a course workspace for managing class activities, student submissions, and teacher feedback. This policy explains how the workspace handles personal information.

Information we collect

Rizal Life stores the information needed to provide the course workspace: name, school email address, account role, class enrollment, task submissions, submitted evidence, feedback, and task-progress status.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate users, organize classes, deliver course invitations, manage tasks and submissions, and allow teachers to review student progress. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Email invitations

Teachers may invite students using an email address supplied for their class. Invitation emails are transactional account-access messages, not marketing messages. Students may receive a replacement invitation if a teacher resends one.

Who can access information

Students can access their own course information. Teachers can access the information of students enrolled in their classes. Authorized project administrators maintain the technical service and database.

Data security and retention

Rizal Life uses authenticated access controls and stores application data through its configured service providers. Course information is retained while it is needed for the class or project, subject to an instructor or administrator's retention requirements.

Questions or requests

For questions about this policy, your account, or a request to correct or remove course information, contact your course instructor or the Rizal Life project administrator.
